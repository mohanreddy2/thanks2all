My journey — thanks to all who shaped my life

I am Mohan Reddy Yadamuri. I was born in a small village. This site is my thank-you to every school, teacher, friend, colleague, and mentor who helped me grow — from Chittoor to Tirupati, Hyderabad, Jakarta, and Singapore.

Read the diary See the full path

I began my early schooling in Bapababana, where I studied until 2nd class. Life moved quickly, and so did my education. I later joined Kazi Patasala in Chittoor, followed by a short period of about two months at Vadhappa Naidu High School.

Our family then moved to Damal Cheruvu, and I continued at ZP High School, completing my schooling there. After that I joined Vidyathi Junior College for +1, and later SRK Junior College, where I completed Intermediate (+1 and +2).

With a wish to build a career in technology, I pursued B.Sc. Computer Science at SV Arts College, Tirupati. After graduation I moved to Hyderabad and completed my MCA at Osmania University.

That decision changed my life.

Soon after MCA I began professional work — a path that has grown into 18+ years across Oracle, SQL Server, PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, cloud platforms, DevOps, and enterprise banking. The work took me across India, Indonesia, and Singapore, with teams at TechMahindra, HCL, GlobalFoundries, UBS, Oracle, SingTel, and now OCBC.

Every school, every teacher, every friend, every colleague, and every mentor played a role. This website is how I say thank you.
